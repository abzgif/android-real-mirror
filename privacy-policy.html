Privacy Policy for Real Mirror

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

Summary: Real Mirror is an open-source, offline utility application. We do not collect, transmit, store on external servers, or sell any of your personal data, camera feeds, photos, or audio recordings. Everything is processed strictly on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Real Mirror ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by abzgif, handles your information when you use our Android mobile application.

We believe in privacy by design. Real Mirror operates completely offline and does not require an account, internet access, or registration to function.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect any personal data.

  • No Analytics: We do not include any tracking software, third-party analytics SDKs, or diagnostic telemetry.
  • No Advertisements: The app is 100% ad-free and contains no advertising networks or trackers.
  • No Cloud Storage: All captured photos and recorded videos are stored locally on your device only.

3. Device Permissions and How They Are Used

Real Mirror requires certain device permissions to deliver its core functionality. Below is an explanation of each permission requested:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA):
    Used exclusively to display the live front-camera feed for the mirror viewfinder (allowing you to see both standard mirrored and true non-reversed reflections) and to take photos or record videos when you tap the capture button. The live camera stream is processed entirely in memory on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.
  • Microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO):
    Used exclusively to record audio during video recording when you choose to record a video. Audio recording only occurs when an active video recording is initiated by you. We never record audio in the background or transmit audio off your device.
  • Storage (android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 9 and older):
    On legacy Android versions (Android 9.0 and below), this permission is required by the Android operating system to save your captured photos and videos to your device's gallery. On Android 10 and newer, photos and videos are saved directly to public MediaStore folders (Pictures/RealMirror and Movies/RealMirror) without requiring storage permissions. Real Mirror does not read, access, or modify your existing photos or personal files.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Real Mirror does not collect or transmit any data to servers:

  • All media (photos and videos) created with Real Mirror is saved directly into your device's photo gallery/storage.
  • You maintain full control over these files. You may delete them at any time using your preferred gallery or file manager app.
  • Uninstalling the app will not automatically delete the photos or videos saved in your device's public media library.

5. Third-Party Services and Links

Real Mirror does not use any third-party SDKs, services, or APIs that collect user data. The app contains optional informational links to its public open-source repository on GitHub (github.com/abzgif/Android-Real-Mirror). Interacting with GitHub is governed by GitHub's own privacy policy.

6. Children's Privacy

Real Mirror does not collect or solicit personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or under 16 where applicable). Since the app collects no personal information, it is safe for users of all ages.

7. Security

Because Real Mirror does not transmit any user data over the internet or store data in the cloud, your photos, videos, and camera feed remain protected by your device's own built-in operating system security measures.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if new features are added. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
